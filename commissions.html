Limited availability

Small programming commissions

I take on a limited number of small, clearly scoped programming projects. Send me what you need, and I’ll check whether it is a good fit before discussing the scope and price with you. Submitting a request does not mean it has been accepted.

You’ll normally receive a response within 24 hours, often sooner depending on availability.

What I Accept

  • Small websites and landing pages
  • Portfolio pages
  • Small scripts and tools
  • Bug fixes
  • UI cleanup
  • Discord or Telegram bot features
  • README/GitHub setup
  • Small automation
  • Focused edits to existing code

What I Don’t Accept

  • Full SaaS or startup builds
  • Large or long-term projects
  • Payment systems
  • Crypto, gambling, banking, or similarly risky projects
  • Malware, spam tools, account bots, or private-data scraping
  • Vague requests without a clear scope

How It Works

  1. 1

    Request

    You send a request through the form below or message me privately. Describe what you need in as much detail as you can.

  2. 2

    Fit Check

    I review whether the project matches what I take on and whether I have time for it. Requests can be declined at this stage.

  3. 3

    Scope & Quote

    If it’s a good fit, we agree on exactly what gets built and you receive an individual quote in EUR, including how many revision rounds are covered.

  4. 4

    Payment Agreement

    We agree on how payment works before any work starts. Small projects are paid after the preview; larger projects start with a 50% deposit. PayPal is the only supported payment method right now.

  5. 5

    Development

    I build the project according to the agreed scope. Any delivery date I mention is an estimate unless explicitly agreed otherwise.

  6. 6

    Preview & Revisions

    You get a preview of the work and can report issues or use the revision rounds included in your quote.

  7. 7

    Final Payment & Delivery

    Once everything is approved, final payment is made and you receive the deliverables we agreed on. Final files, source code, and repository access are handed over after full payment.

  8. 8

    7-Day Support

    For seven days after delivery, I fix reproducible bugs that break functionality included in the agreed scope, free of charge.

Pricing & Payment

Pricing depends entirely on the scope of the project, so there are no public starting prices. Every accepted request gets an individual quote.

All prices and payments use EUR. PayPal is the only supported payment method for now. Whether a commission counts as small or larger is decided during the fit check and is part of the quote.

Small projects

  • No upfront payment.
  • I build the work and show you a preview.
  • You pay the full amount once you’re happy with the preview.
  • Final files, source code, repository access, or ownership are delivered after full payment.

Larger projects

  • 50% is paid before work begins.
  • You get a preview when the work is ready.
  • The remaining 50% is paid before final delivery.
  • Final files, source code, repository access, or ownership are delivered after full payment.

Revisions

Two revision rounds are included by default. Depending on the project, more than two may be included — the exact allowance is always stated in the quote.

Additional revisions beyond what’s included may cost extra. New features or changes outside the agreed scope are not revisions and may require a separate quote.

Delivery

The delivery method depends on the project and is agreed in the quote. Possible delivery methods include:

  • Source files
  • ZIP archive
  • GitHub repository access
  • Repository ownership transfer
  • Deployment handoff
  • Setup instructions or documentation when agreed

Before You Request

  • Requests may be accepted or declined.
  • Scope and price are agreed before work begins.
  • Two revision rounds are included by default.
  • Extra features or revisions may cost more.
  • Final files are delivered after full payment.
  • Bug-fix support lasts seven days after final delivery.
