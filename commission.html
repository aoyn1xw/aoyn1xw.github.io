Services and fit

Good fit

  • Small websites, landing pages, and portfolios
  • Scripts and automation
  • Focused bug fixes
  • UI cleanup and small frontend changes
  • Bot features
  • README and GitHub setup
  • Small edits to existing projects

Not a fit

  • Full SaaS products, startup builds, or large platforms
  • Login or payment systems
  • Maintenance-heavy work unless explicitly agreed
  • Crypto or gambling systems
  • Malware, spam, account bots, or private-data scraping
  • Vague requests such as “build me an app like Discord”

How it works

  1. 01

    Request

    You explain the project and budget.

  2. 02

    Review and quote

    I check fit, ask questions, and send scope, price, and estimated timing.

  3. 03

    Agreement and payment

    Work starts only after we accept the plan and any required payment is received.

  4. 04

    Development

    I build the agreed scope and communicate meaningful delays.

  5. 05

    Review

    You review the result and can use up to two included revision rounds.

  6. 06

    Delivery and support

    Final files are delivered after required payment, followed by seven days of bug-fix support.

Practical terms

  • A request is not automatic acceptance.
  • We agree a fixed scope and quote before work begins.
  • The payment method and timing are agreed upfront; tiny jobs may be paid fully upfront, while larger jobs may use a deposit.
  • Up to two reasonable revision rounds are included unless agreed otherwise.
  • Major scope changes or new features can require a new quote.
  • Final custom deliverables transfer after full payment.
  • Seven days of bug-fix support are included after final delivery.
