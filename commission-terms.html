Small programming commissions

Commission Terms

Last updated: August 11, 2026

These Commission Terms apply to small programming and development commissions requested through this website or linked contact methods. By accepting a quote and commissioning a project, you agree to these terms.

1. Commission Requests

Submitting a commission request does not mean the commission has been accepted.

I may accept or decline any request at my discretion, including if the project is too large, unclear, outside my skill set, outside my availability, legally risky, or simply not a good fit.

I mainly accept small, clearly scoped programming requests. I do not accept full startup builds, complex infrastructure, large platforms, payment systems, or ongoing maintenance-heavy projects unless explicitly agreed otherwise.

2. Scope

The agreed scope defines what will be built.

A commission only includes the features, deliverables, and work explicitly agreed before development begins. Anything outside that scope may require a separate quote.

Examples of suitable commissions include small websites, landing pages, portfolio pages, small scripts, bug fixes, UI cleanup, bot features, README/GitHub setup, small automation tools, and code edits for existing projects.

Examples of unsuitable commissions include full SaaS platforms, large apps, complete startup builds, complex infrastructure, login/payment systems, crypto or gambling-related systems, malware, spam tools, account bots, private-data scraping, or vague requests like “build me an app like Discord.”

3. Quotes

Once the requirements are clear, I may provide a quote.

The quote may include the agreed scope, price, estimated delivery time, revision limit, and delivery method.

A quote is only valid for the scope discussed at that time. If the request changes significantly, the quote may also change.

4. Payment

Payment details are handled individually after a quote has been accepted.

Depending on the size and nature of the project, I may require full payment upfront or a deposit before development begins.

Development normally begins only after the agreed initial payment has been received.

Final source code, unrestricted files, repository transfer, or other final deliverables may be withheld until the full agreed amount has been paid.

Do not add an automated payment button to this page. Payment must stay manual for now.

5. Development

I will develop the project according to the agreed scope.

Any delivery estimate is only an estimate unless explicitly agreed otherwise.

If a significant delay happens, I will communicate it as soon as reasonably possible.

6. Review and Revisions

Before final delivery, I may provide a preview, demo, screenshots, video, hosted version, test build, or another reasonable way to review the project.

Unless otherwise agreed, a commission includes up to two reasonable revision rounds.

A revision means an adjustment to something already included in the agreed scope.

A request for substantial new functionality, a redesign, or a major change in direction is not a revision. It is new scope and may require a new quote.

7. Delivery

After the project has been approved and all required payments have been received, I will provide the agreed deliverables.

Delivery may include source files, a ZIP archive, a GitHub repository, repository transfer, deployment, or another agreed method.

The exact delivery method depends on the individual commission.

8. Bug-Fix Support

Unless otherwise agreed, I provide a 7-day bug-fix support period beginning on the date of final delivery.

During this period, I will fix reproducible bugs that cause agreed functionality to not work as intended.

This does not include new features, requirement changes, problems caused by modifications made by the client or third parties, external service changes, hosting issues, API changes, or issues outside my reasonable control.

This support period does not affect any statutory rights that may apply.

9. Cancellations and Refunds

If a commission is cancelled before development begins, payments made for work that has not started may be refundable.

If development has already begun, any refund depends on the amount of work already completed and any non-recoverable costs incurred for the project.

If I am unable to complete an accepted commission, I will communicate this and provide an appropriate refund for work that cannot be delivered.

Completed and delivered work is generally non-refundable, except where required by applicable law or where the delivered work materially fails to match the agreed scope.

10. Ownership

Unless otherwise agreed, rights to the custom final deliverables created specifically for the commission transfer to the client after full payment.

I retain ownership of pre-existing materials, reusable code, general-purpose components, templates, development tools, workflows, techniques, and anything not created exclusively for that specific commission.

Third-party software, libraries, fonts, assets, APIs, and other external materials remain subject to their own licenses and terms.

11. Portfolio Use

Unless confidentiality has been agreed beforehand, I may display non-sensitive parts of completed work in my portfolio or use them as examples of my development work.

I will not intentionally publish private credentials, personal information, confidential data, private source code, or other information that should reasonably remain private.

If a project must stay private, this needs to be discussed before the commission is accepted.

12. Client Responsibilities

The client is responsible for providing accurate requirements, materials, access, information, and feedback needed to complete the project.

The client must have the necessary rights or permission to provide any assets, code, branding, content, or other materials used in the project.

The client may not commission work intended for unlawful, fraudulent, malicious, abusive, or otherwise prohibited purposes.

13. Third-Party Services

Some projects may depend on third-party platforms, APIs, hosting providers, libraries, payment providers, or other external services.

I am not responsible for outages, pricing changes, account restrictions, API changes, discontinued services, or other third-party actions outside my control.

Any recurring third-party costs are the client’s responsibility unless explicitly agreed otherwise.

14. No Automatic Acceptance

A submitted request, message, or form entry does not create a commission by itself.

A commission only starts once the scope, price, payment terms, and delivery expectations have been discussed and accepted.

15. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated for future commissions.

For an existing commission, the applicable terms are generally the version accepted when that commission was agreed, unless both sides agree otherwise.

16. Questions

If anything is unclear, ask before accepting a quote or making a payment.
